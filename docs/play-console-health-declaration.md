# Play Console Health Apps Declaration

Use this note when resubmitting Anubhav Lifecare after the Play Console warning:

> Inaccurate Health Apps Declaration
> Healthcare Services and Management

## Required Play Console Update

Go to Play Console > App content > Health Apps and declare:

- Medical > Healthcare Services and Management

This category matches the current app because it supports lab test booking, patient booking management, report access/download/share, billing and pending payment tracking, patient record workflows, and collector follow-up workflows.

Do not declare "My app doesn't provide any health features."

## Suggested Health Declaration Explanation

Anubhav Lifecare provides healthcare service management features for patients and authorized collectors. Patients can book lab tests, manage bookings, view bills and pending payments, access lab reports, download report PDFs, and share reports. Collectors can manage referred patient details, patient logs, follow-ups, reports, and booking-related workflows. The app is used for healthcare logistics, health record access, billing, and patient-provider workflow support.

## Suggested Store Listing Disclaimer

Anubhav Lifecare is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Suggested Review Response

This matches the app's features for lab test booking, patient booking management, health report access, report PDF download/share, billing and pending payment tracking, patient records, and collector follow-up workflows. - Privacy policy is publicly accessible, non-PDF, and linked in Play Console.
